## The Problem

A fast-growing media company runs five revenue streams — podcast sponsorships, brand partnerships, consulting, live events, and digital courses — through a single HubSpot pipeline. Leadership has no visibility into which channels actually convert, which deal types stall, or whether closed-won revenue is trending in the right direction.

HubSpot's native reports answer *what happened*. They don't answer *why*, and they can't be queried, joined, or versioned.
